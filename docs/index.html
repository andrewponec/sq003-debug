SQ003 gas simmer controller — debug workspace

E.G.O./Tytronics SQ003-H from a Thermador Professional range: both XtraLow burners dead (no spark, no gas), second failure in ~2 years. Everything reverse-engineered so far lives here — interactive viewers, the netlist, schematic drafts, and the bench protocol. Every claim carries its evidence grade:

M meter-verifiedH human, from photos V visually catalogedCV computer vision DS datasheet / inference

Where the diagnosis stands

The failure signature (both XLO channels dead together, everything else fine, ~2-year repeat) is best matched by the supply-collapse-under-load class — dropper cap aging, reservoir ESR, or rectifier/zener drift. The decisive discriminator is bench test B4: watch the logic rail while the relay + a solenoid load kick in. Full reasoning in the design notes and report.

Interactive tools

X-ray viewer

Front of the board with the back-side copper as a toggleable layer (x to toggle, slider for opacity). Click a net to highlight it; hover shows net identity + evidence.

Copper annotator

Draw along copper runs to assert connectivity; barriers to cut false bridges. Hit 💾 Save when done — corrections flow straight into the netlist pipeline.

Solder lab

Hands-on: knobs for the solder/board edge decision on four board crops — see exactly where and why the edge is hard (WHY map, hover readout).

Net map

The full current merged-net picture as one zoomable image — the review artifact.

Methodology, findings, errata, fault tree, red-team log.

Design notes

The "how would E.G.O. have built this" exercise that fills the hidden-topology gaps, with 11 testable predictions and the PSU failure math.

How to contribute findings

1. Do any subset of the bench guide (A-steps are all safe, board unpowered).
2. Record results in the measurements.yaml format shown at the end of the bench guide (or just write them in an email/text).
3. Draw any copper corrections in the annotator and hit 💾 Save.
Every measurement upgrades the netlist and regenerates the schematic automatically.
